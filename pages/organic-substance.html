VietnameseChất hữu cơ
EnglishOrganic substance / compound
Pronunciation/ɔːrˈɡænɪk ˈsʌbstəns/ / /ˈkɑːmpaʊnd/
Word typeDanh từ (Noun)

Meaning (Vietnamese)

Hợp chất của carbon (trừ CO, CO₂, muối carbonate, cyanide, carbide...), có thể chứa thêm H, O, N, S, halogen...

Meaning (English)

An organic substance is generally understood to be a carbon compound that may also contain hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, sulfur, halogens, and other elements, with exceptions like CO, CO₂, carbonates, cyanides, carbides.

Công thức phân tử

Pronunciation & Word type

/məˈlekjələr ˈfɔːrmjələ/ · Noun phrase

Meaning

A formula showing types and numbers of atoms in a molecule; expresses the exact number of atoms of each element in a molecule.

Example

Although glucose and fructose have different structures, they share the same molecular formula, C₆H₁₂O₆.

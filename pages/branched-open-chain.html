Meaning (Vietnamese)

Mạch carbon không khép kín thành vòng nhưng có nhánh phụ.

Meaning (English)

A carbon chain that is not closed into a ring and has one or more side branches.

Another definition from the notes

Mạch carbon hở có các nhóm carbon rẽ nhánh ra khỏi mạch chính.

An open carbon chain that contains one or more carbon side branches attached to the main chain.

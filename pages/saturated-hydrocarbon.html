Meaning (Vietnamese)

Hiđrocacbon no là hiđrocacbon chỉ chứa các liên kết đơn giữa các nguyên tử cacbon.

Meaning (English)

a hydrocarbon in which the carbon atoms are joined to as many hydrogen atoms as possible.

Example

Alkanes are saturated hydrocarbons.

Ankan là các hiđrocacbon no.

Another definition from the notes

A hydrocarbon containing only single covalent bonds between carbon atoms.
